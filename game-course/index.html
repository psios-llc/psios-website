Why most game projects die

You can code.
That was never the problem.

Roblox has a hundred million daily players and a toolchain that fights newcomers in specific, predictable ways. This course is built around those specific failures.

  • Studio keeps your game in a binary file, so Git is useless until you fix that — most people never do, and lose work.
  • Luau looks like Python until it isn't, and the object model decides whether your script runs on the server, the client, or silently never.
  • Scope eats the project in week two. The game gets bigger every time you sit down, and it never gets finished.
  • AI agents happily generate a thousand lines of plausible nonsense when you have not told them what the world's rules are.
  • Exploiters are real, data loss is real, and phones are where your players actually are — all three are usually discovered at launch.

Each of those has a day assigned to it, in the order you will actually hit them. The deadline is the method: a daily lesson with one build task, and a ship check at the end of every week.
