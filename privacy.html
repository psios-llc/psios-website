Privacy

Short, because there is little to say.

psios.com is a static website. It does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking pixels, and it has no form backend.

The inquiry form prepares an email draft in your own email app. Nothing you type is stored by this site or sent anywhere until you send that email yourself.

If you email derek@psios.com, your message is kept only for the purpose of replying and, if we work together, for the engagement itself.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address when the font files are requested.
