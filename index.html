Case study · Psios internal

The assistant that
runs this company.

Psios is a one-person company. The reason it works is a resident AI assistant — install 01 above — running on a Mac Mini in the office since mid-2026.

The problem

One founder, several ventures, and a growing pile of notes, decisions, and follow-ups that no single person can keep in their head — plus the daily admin of running a company alone.

What was installed

  • An open-source agent on an always-on Mac Mini, reached through Telegram
  • A company knowledge base the assistant reads and maintains itself
  • A weekly scheduled job that files new material, checks the knowledge base for gaps, and commits the result
  • Voice input, so questions can be asked hands-free

What changed

Decisions, contacts, and project state are asked for, not searched for. Routine upkeep happens on a schedule instead of when someone remembers. The founder's time goes to clients and product.

Numbers on hours saved are deliberately not claimed. Ask Derek for a live walkthrough instead.
